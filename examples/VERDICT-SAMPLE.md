# Example verdict — illustrative, NOT an executed AI jury

**Case:** Launch a paid course before validating demand.

**Charge:** The proposal should not proceed exactly as stated.

**Method:** Demonstration only. The following synthetic positions were written for documentation. They are not actual independent agent votes, research findings or test results.

## Jury votes (fictional example with 3 perspectives)

| Juror | Vote | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| The Accountant | GUILTY | Three months of work before a revenue test presents avoidable opportunity cost. |
| The First-Time Customer | GUILTY | Nobody has demonstrated willingness to pay for this course. |
| The Engineer | NOT GUILTY | The instructor has a feasible amount of time and a limited direct cash budget. |

## Charges that stuck

The plan commits substantial development time without validated demand. Its price and acquisition channel are untested. A smaller pilot is a concrete alternative.

## What the defense got right

The instructor has relevant teaching experience and a small cash budget.

## Conditions for acquittal

1. Offer a paid pilot and record actual conversion results.
2. Interview prospective customers and document the specific learning problem.
3. Define a small initial curriculum and an explicit stop/go criterion.

## The sentence

Test willingness to pay with a pilot before committing three months to full production.
