# Common decision rubric

All specialists assess the **proposal exactly as stated**, not its abstract potential.

- **Evidence**: What is actually observed? Flag user assertions and unsupported claims. No invented metrics, citations or competitor facts.
- **Materiality**: Could this issue change the go/no-go decision? Focus on severity, not rhetorical volume.
- **Reversibility**: Prefer reversible experiments for unknowns; call out irreversible costs and risks.
- **Risk**: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or UNKNOWN; risk is qualitative, not a calibrated probability.
- **Actionability**: Supply a concrete experiment, measurable threshold or missing piece of evidence that could change the vote.
- **Trade-offs**: Note the opportunity cost and best realistic alternative.

`GUILTY` = should not proceed as **currently stated**. `NOT GUILTY` = reasonable to proceed under stated safeguards and evidence. Tie = `HUNG JURY`. `NO VOTE` is missing/invalid and does not count in either camp. Vote shares are **not** failure probabilities; roles are prompted model perspectives, even if context-separated.

Per-juror output fields: `VOTE`, `REASON`, `DECIDING FACT`, `WOULD FLIP IF`, `EXPERIMENT`, `RISK`. Do not modify machine keys. Answers can be Persian.
